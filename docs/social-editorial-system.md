# Social Editorial System

## Purpose

Social distribution should create demand for Gerardo's two distinct practices
and reinforce the doctrine that connects them. It must not collapse Crittora,
WUN/XEO Labs, and APP into a single generic AI offer.

## Editorial Mix

- **60% Mortgage AI governance:** consequential mortgage workflows, authority
  perimeters, controls, evidence, oversight, and implementation. Core question:
  “What does this mortgage AI action have permission to do—and how do you prove
  it?”
- **25% AI visibility:** AEO, SEO, cross-engine visibility, content systems,
  conversion surfaces, and approval-led optimization. Core question: “Being
  indexed is not the same as being understood by AI.”
- **15% Founder doctrine:** authority, evidence, trust, and the operating
  implications that connect governed action with reliable public information.

## Post Pattern

Each LinkedIn or X post should contain:

1. A concrete operating question or observation.
2. The plain-language implication for a mortgage or high-trust business.
3. One proof point, framework, or practical next action.
4. A lane-specific CTA: Crittora for mortgage governance, WUN/XEO Labs for
   visibility, or Gerardo's site/contact form for doctrine and advisory work.

Use Crittora as the source of truth for mortgage-specific claims and XEO Labs
as the source of truth for cross-engine methodology claims. Do not claim a
formal MISMO designation unless formal approved wording is supplied.
