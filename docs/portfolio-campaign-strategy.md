# Campaign Strategy: Governed AI & Trusted Visibility

## Positioning

Gerardo I. Ornelas is a systems architect, founder, and advisor working at the
two points where AI-era businesses win or fail: the authority to act and the
ability to be understood.

The personal site is the senior strategic layer above two concrete practices:

1. **Control:** mortgage AI governance, authority perimeters, evidence,
   oversight, and implementation through Crittora.
2. **Discoverability:** AEO, SEO, cross-engine visibility, content systems,
   and approval-led optimization through WUN AEO Director and XEO Labs.
3. **Doctrine:** APP, essays, briefings, speaking, and advisory work explaining
   why high-trust AI needs governed action and reliable public understanding.

## Audience and Conversion

- Mortgage enterprises, consultancies, technology platforms, and AI builders
  responsible for consequential mortgage workflows should reach Crittora.
- Regional and high-trust businesses that need credible visibility across
  search and answer engines should reach WUN/XEO Labs.
- Leaders seeking research, speaking, or senior advisory context should use
  Gerardo's briefing form.

The site must make the practice boundary explicit on every conversion path.
Crittora remains exclusively mortgage-focused. The personal site must neither
become a generic AI consultancy nor a pure Crittora founder page.

## Site System

- Homepage: lead with “AI needs permission to act. Businesses need clarity to
  be found.” Then direct visitors to mortgage governance, visibility strategy,
  or a briefing.
- Practices: order Crittora first, visibility second, and doctrine third.
- Research: retain authority-layer, manifesto, UI-GATES, and related articles as
  doctrine and supporting proof, not as the primary commercial offer.
- Legacy ventures: do not use Violetek or Qripton Verify in primary navigation,
  SEO, structured data, or conversion paths; retain reachable historical URLs
  only when continuity warrants it.
- Search: use person and page metadata that connects mortgage AI governance,
  controls/evidence, AEO/SEO, and cross-engine visibility.

## Content and Distribution

Use the 60/25/15 social mix defined in `docs/social-editorial-system.md`.
Long-form writing should alternate among mortgage governance field notes,
visibility systems guidance, and founder doctrine. Each asset must identify the
practice it represents and send readers to the corresponding destination.

MISMO FRAME may be cited as a relevant practical framework for mortgage AI
governance. Do not claim a certification, advisory-partner designation, or
other formal MISMO relationship without exact approved wording.

## Success Signals

- Qualified mortgage governance conversations and Crittora referrals.
- Qualified visibility assessments and WUN/XEO Labs referrals.
- Briefing requests, speaking invitations, and citations of the authority and
  evidence doctrine.
- Growth in relevant branded search, direct traffic, social-to-site clicks, and
  returning readers—without confusing the practices in inbound conversations.
